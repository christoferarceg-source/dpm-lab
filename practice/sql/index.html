Week 1 · The Bullseye · Nobody trusts the number

See the rows behind the number

Warm-up

Before you argue about a conversion rate, look at the rows that feed it.

Return the deal_id, account_id, and amount of every deal whose stage is closed_won, ordered by deal_id.

Why this matters for a data product

Filtering to closed_won is the first step of the functional metric deals_closed_value. A Data PM who can point at the exact rows behind an aggregate can defend it; one who can't is trusting the dashboard.
