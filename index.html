DPM Lab

Your first six weeks as Meridian's first Data Product Manager.

A 300-person B2B software company selling field-operations software to mid-size and enterprise customers. Sales runs on a CRM, finance runs on invoices, and until now nobody has owned the data in between. Each week you get a situation, the framework it needs, decisions to make, and the SQL to prove your answer. The data has real problems planted in it. Find them.
